Can a machine hear how you feel?

Record a sentence with feeling: angry, sad, happy, afraid, disgusted, bored or neutral. Two CNNs, trained on the German EmoDB corpus and evaluated on speakers they never heard, give their answer. Everything runs in your browser, and no audio leaves your computer.
